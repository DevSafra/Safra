# The partnership agreement, from generation to signature

**Bashar, 2026-08-21.** Step 4 of «انضم كشريك»: SAFRA draws up the agreement, both sides sign it,
and only then is the partner approved.

## Signing is on PAPER

**Electronic signatures are not accepted in Syria**, which is where the partners are. An earlier
design of this used a typed-name simple electronic signature; it was stopped for that reason. So the
platform carries what the parties actually do — print, sign, scan, send — and its job is to order
the steps, hold the files and record what happened.

That is why there is no "I agree" button anywhere in this flow, and why every transition is an
upload.

## The states, and whose turn each one is

| Status                       | What it means                                  | Who acts next |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| `draft`                      | Generated by SAFRA. Nobody has signed.         | **SAFRA**     |
| `awaiting_partner_signature` | SAFRA's signed scan is in. Partner can see it. | **Partner**   |
| `active`                     | Both scans are in. The agreement is in force.  | —             |
| `superseded`                 | Replaced by a newer contract of the same kind. | —             |
| `terminated`                 | Ended.                                         | —             |

Generating again supersedes whatever was live, **including a draft** — so a corrected version
replaces the old one rather than leaving two contracts of the same kind in play. The console only
offers «إنشاء نسخة جديدة» while the contract is still a draft: regenerating after it has been sent
would supersede a document the partner may already be signing, and the platform has no way to tell
them.

## The steps

1. **Generate** — «إنشاء العقد» on the partner's page in the console. The document is rendered from
   `contract-template.ts`, hashed, and stored as a `draft`.
2. **Download and sign** — «تنزيل العقد», print, sign by hand, scan.
3. **Upload SAFRA's copy** — «ارفع نسخة سفرة الموقّعة». This moves the contract to
   `awaiting_partner_signature` **and emails the partner**.
4. **The partner does the same** — the contract appears on «العقود والمستندات» in their portal with
   a download link and an upload form. Their upload makes it `active` **and emails every active
   super admin**.
5. **Approve the partner.** A missing contract is advisory, not blocking — see below.

## The wording is a PLACEHOLDER

`CLAUSES` in `apps/api/src/admin/contract-template.ts` ships with draft clauses, and the rendered
document carries a red banner in both languages saying they have not been reviewed by counsel.
**Replacing the wording is an edit to that one array**, and removing the banner is the last step of
finishing it.

The commercial terms are NOT in the template: commission, the customer fee and the notice period are
read from settings at generation and interpolated. A contract stating a rate the platform does not
charge is worse than no contract, and the surest way to produce one is to type the number into the
document.

## What the record proves, and what it does not

Each upload writes one `partner_contract_signatures` row: which party, which account uploaded it,
the file and its hash, the IP and the time, and `original_hash` — the generated version that was
current when the scan came back.

**`original_hash` is the one worth understanding.** A scan is a photograph of paper: nothing about
its bytes can be checked against the generated PDF, because the whole point is that it went through
a printer and a pen. What the platform can say is _which version each side was signing_, so a
partner who signed a superseded revision is a discrepancy the record shows rather than hides.

The platform does **not** verify that a signature is genuine, or that the scan is of the document it
claims to be. That is a human judgement, and the files are kept so a human can make it.

## Approval is not blocked by a missing contract

Deliberate, and the same shape as the sanctions policy: the console warns «لا يوجد عقد ساري لهذا
الشريك بعد» next to the approval control, and the button works. A missing contract must not strand
onboarding the way the sanctions feed once did.

## Production requirement

Generation prints HTML in headless Chromium, so **the API image needs the browser**. `O-ops-1` in
`FUTURE-WORK.md` records it. Without it the service starts, every other route works, and the first
symptom is «إنشاء العقد» failing.
